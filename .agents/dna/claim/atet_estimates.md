# CEO replacement has small ATET on firm outcomes

## Claim

Replacing the CEO raises firm outcomes by a small amount: ATET 0.005 on growth, 0.001 on assets, 0.003 on capital at 0.1% significance over the event window, per the application paper table 2.

## Evidence

table2.tex reports the ATET estimates and 3,361,459 observations; significance stars at 0.1%.

The application paper is the older-era analysis; the econometrics paper is the active July 2026 version.
